These are what those columns mean:
ara_abbrev: the allen reference atlas (allen brain atlas) abbreviation for the given region. It is not unique for each hemisphere.

gn_symbol: 3 letter code and ‘-B’, ‘-R’, or ‘-L’ indicating the hemisphere. If the region is considered “uncharted” no abbreviation will exist here.  All capitals are primarily grey matter. All lower case is white matter tract. Starts with a capital but rest is lower case is a ventricle.

hemisphere_assignment: indicates Left (-1), Right (+1), or Bilateral (0) for hemisphere as a quick way to filter the data.

id32_fsabi and id64_fsabi: are two forms (32 bit and 64 bit) to represent the regional data. Typically not used for filtering

roi: unique number assigned to each region in the atlas. Numbers between 1 and 180 indicate left hemisphere child regions, Numbers between 1001 and 1180 indicate right hemisphere child regions. Any other number here is a region made by combining various children which may or may not exist in this dataset.

structure: a combination of the gn_symbol code for the region without the hemisphere indicate and the full name of the region in DMBA atlas with a spelling out of the hemisphere for left and right hemisphere responses, bilateral just doesn't indicate hemisphere there.

structure_id: is the code in the allen reference atlas for the given region. Typically not used for filtering


This column exists in both group_data and group_stats.
['stratification']

This is what that column means:
stratification: indicates what hemisphere the grouping or analysis was done on (typically we only perform full analysis on bilateral) so the column will read at least hemisphere=bilateral. In many cases this additionally gives information about if stratification was done on the data prior to analysis. The column could read hemisphere=bilateral Age_of_Termination=2 which would indicate this is the bilateral data for a 2 month age of termination.

This column exists in both group_data and subject_data.
['genotype']

This is what that column means:
genotype: is a user defined grouping from the model (you can see this as a term in the source_of_variation indicated in group stats). In group_data, it indicates all the groupings underneath genotype (HET and WILD) and indicating no delineation for the grouping '-'. In subject_data, it indicates what genotype the specific specimen has (HET or WILD).

Typically, the contrasts of FA (fractional anisotropy), AD (axial diffusivity), RD (radial diffusivity), MD (mean diffusivity), volume_mm3 (also called absolute volume), and volume_fraction (also called fractional volume or normalized volume) are considered here.
